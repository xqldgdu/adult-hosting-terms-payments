## What an adult-friendly hosting contract looks like

Adult web hosting is ordinary hosting sold by a company whose terms accept adult content. The servers are the same as any other host's. What changes is the contract, and hosts that have not put the permission in writing keep the right to suspend a site over nudity, pornography or gambling.

Four conditions show up again and again in the policies that do allow it:

1. **Legal where the server sits and where you operate.** Hostinger names Indonesia, where pornography is prohibited, as an example of a jurisdiction whose law applies to content on its network.
2. **No copyright infringement.** Ripped tube clips and unlicensed image sets get accounts closed faster than explicit material does.
3. **No minors, in any form.** This includes drawings and simulated content on most hosts.
4. **No gambling scripts.** Hostinger permits informational and affiliate gambling sites but blocks casino games, poker, slots and bingo software.

Hostinger is a useful benchmark because its rules are unusually explicit. It accepts pornography and hentai when the material is legal, copyright-compliant and free of minors, and accepts erotic video, images and writing under the same terms. Its AI tools, including the WordPress assistant and AI Builder, will not generate adult content. A site that breaks the rules can be suspended without notice, and Hostinger will not migrate it. That last clause tells you how final the decision is.

DreamHost works differently. Its acceptable-use policy is the final authority when a dispute arises. ## How much storage and bandwidth your site actually needs

Published testing guidance puts the floor at 40GB of storage, with 100GB or more if you expect to grow, an uptime guarantee of at least 99.9%, and page loads under three seconds. Those numbers are reasonable, and they are also where budget plans quietly fail.

Three rough tiers cover most adult projects:

- **Text plus a few hundred images:** 25GB is enough. Backup size and inode limits matter more than raw disk.
- **A gallery of several thousand images, or a membership area with thumbnails:** 50GB to 100GB.
- **Any video you host yourself:** a VPS with a 1Gbps port and unmetered or very generous transfer.

To make the video number concrete: a 10-minute 1080p clip at 5 Mbps is roughly 375MB. Fifty hosted clips plus thumbnails and backups already exceed a 15GB plan. That is arithmetic, not a benchmark, but it explains why video sites outgrow shared hosting in weeks.

## How adult sites get paid, and why your host cannot fix it

This is the part the ranking articles leave out. You can buy the perfect server and still be unable to take a single payment.

Hostinger does not offer a PCI DSS compliant environment, so storing card numbers on the server is off the table. That is true almost everywhere; the workaround is a hosted checkout page from a payment provider, never a self-hosted card form.

Mainstream processors are the real bottleneck. Wix Payments refuses adult goods outright, so an adult store built on Wix still needs a third-party gateway. Most conventional merchant acquirers restrict adult merchants by policy, which is why the sector relies on specialist processors such as CCBill and Segpay. Budget for the application process and expect underwriting questions about your content, your age gate and your refund policy.

Age verification is now a legal requirement in much of the US. After the Texas age-verification law survived a Supreme Court challenge, more than 20 states adopted similar rules for commercial sites where roughly a third of the content is harmful to minors. No host performs this check for you; Wix's own guidance places the age-gating duty on the site owner.

US producers also carry record-keeping duties under 18 U.S.C. § 2257, which covers identification records for performers and how long they are kept. If you are producing original content rather than licensing it, read that statute before your first upload, not after your first complaint.
